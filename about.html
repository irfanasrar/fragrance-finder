About & accessibility

I made this for my Web Technologies module at Sheffield Hallam. You tell it what you like, where you're going and your city, and it picks some perfumes for you.

Where the data comes from

Perfumes come from Open Beauty Facts (ODbL). The brand list on the Finder is from Wikidata (CC0). The weather comes from Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0). Scent family names and notes are in my own scent-families.json file. If one of the APIs is down, the site uses a backup list so it still works.

Accessibility

I tried to follow the W3C WCAG guidelines. The colours are just black and greys so the contrast passes AA.

Every page has a skip link to jump to the main content. The pages use proper landmarks and headings, and every form field has a label. The Finder groups its questions in fieldsets.

If you get something wrong on the form, the error shows next to the field and in a list at the top. Results are read out by screen readers through a live status message.

You can use it all with a keyboard. Each result card is one link, focus outlines show where you are, and Escape closes the mobile menu. Product photos have alt text, and there's a plain placeholder when a photo is missing. Animations are turned off if you've set reduced motion.
